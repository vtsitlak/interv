AI-powered interviews

Your profile. Your AI twin. Better interviews.

Interv helps candidates train an AI twin from their experience and lets recruiters run async interviews via a public profile link or in-app search. Match scores, feedback, and optional contact requests keep both sides moving without calendar coordination.

For candidates

Sign in, train your twin from CV text, links, and personal Q&A, then manage everything from your dashboard — including who can find you and whether your public link is active.

  • Train and edit your profile anytime
  • Share a public link so anyone can start an interview
  • Disable the public link or hide from recruiter search separately
  • Practice with a test interview before going live
  • Review match scores, feedback, and contact requests on your dashboard

For recruiters

Create a recruiter account, complete your profile, then search published candidates and interview their AI twin — no setup form on each interview. Leave a match score, notes, and optionally share your email so strong matches can reach out.

  • Search candidates by name, role, or skills
  • See who you have already interviewed from search and profiles
  • Start, restart, or review interviews with match scores and transcripts
  • Update feedback anytime from your dashboard or candidate profile

How it works

1

Train the twin

Candidates add CV text, links, and Q&A so the AI answers with their experience and tone.

2

Run the interview

Someone opens the candidate's public link and starts an interview, or a signed-in recruiter finds them in search and runs a session from the app.

3

Match score & follow up

After the interview, the recruiter leaves a match score and feedback. They can optionally share their email; the candidate sees everything on their dashboard and can keep improving their twin.
