AI-powered interviews

Your profile. Your AI twin. Better interviews.

Interv helps candidates build an AI representation of their experience and lets recruiters discover profiles, run structured interviews, and leave scores and feedback—without scheduling back-and-forth.

For candidates

Train your AI twin from your CV, links, and personal Q&A, then share a public profile or keep it hidden from recruiter search until you are ready.

  • Train and edit your profile anytime
  • Practice with a test interview on your dashboard
  • Control recruiter discoverability from your dashboard
  • See interview feedback and scores in one place

For recruiters

Sign in, set up your recruiter profile, search published candidates, and run interviews with their AI twin. Review transcripts, score each session, and update feedback from your dashboard.

  • Search candidates by name, role, or skills
  • Start or restart interviews from a candidate profile
  • Score and feedback saved per interview
  • Dashboard with interview history and transcripts

How it works

1

Train the twin

Candidates add CV text, links, and Q&A so the AI answers with their experience and tone.

2

Run the interview

Recruiters find candidates in search or open a shared link, then chat with the AI twin in a guided session.

3

Score & iterate

Recruiters leave a score and notes; candidates see feedback on their dashboard and can improve their profile.
